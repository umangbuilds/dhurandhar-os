---

## Why this is a stub

DhurandharOS v0.1 prioritised **operator-fundamentals** (PRD, Builder, Reviewer, Idea Reality Check, Hiring) over launch playbooks. The reasoning:

1. Operators with a working product but no launch playbook can install Corey's kit today and ship.
2. Operators without a working product won't benefit from a launch skill — Idea Reality Check + PRD Writer + Builder is what they need first.
3. Building a DhurandharOS-voiced launch skill takes deliberate work — Indian channel mix, low-trust market patterns, founder-led GTM in a non-VC-funded context. v0.2 will do this properly.
