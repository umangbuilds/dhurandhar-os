# Mission

DhurandharOS exists because the senior team is no longer the bottleneck — the senior workflow is.

A non-technical operator with 13 years of fintech under their belt, building alone, should not have to choose between hiring a CTO they cannot afford and shipping vibe-coded software that breaks at the first regulatory inspection. There is a third option: encode the senior team into a workflow the operator drives.

That is the work.

---

## Why now

Three things are true at the same time for the first time:

1. The discipline of senior product, engineering, security, and hiring practice has been written down — much of it as open-source skills inside the Claude Code ecosystem and adjacent communities.
2. A non-technical operator can drive that discipline through Claude Code as the primary execution layer. The operator does not need to write the function from scratch; they need to know what the function should do.
3. Building serious products alone — with no co-founder, no CTO, no engineering team — has stopped being the exception. It is the new default for operators who have range, taste, and the will to ship.

The senior team becomes a file the operator loads. The workflow becomes the moat.

---

## The 90-day arc

DhurandharOS is a 90-day demonstration project. The arc is honest:

- **v0.1 (Day 1).** Six Tier 1 skills + one stub. Universal solo-operator workflow. Soul Keeper anchors voice and memory (with SessionStart hook precedence). Builder runs TDD-first with mandatory post-build review. Reviewer runs adversarial. PRD Writer is money-aware. Idea Reality Check refuses to ship code without three customers. Deployment Advisor names the migration triggers from Supabase pre-revenue to AWS Mumbai for regulated production.
- **v0.2 (Day 60–90).** Launch + Marketer expanded from stub. Hiring JD Writer ships (deferred from v0.1) — comp bands from public listings, never from competitors. India fintech additions land in core: DPDPA Compliance, RBI Digital Lending, UPI Payments. The fintech operator's brain comes online.
- **v0.3 (Day 90–180).** Broader Indian compliance — incorporation, GST/TDS, POSH, ESOP, FEMA, ToS/Privacy. Hindi/regional content variants. Tier 2/3 GTM playbooks.

Each version is shippable on its own. The operator using v0.1 today should have shipped a real product by the time v0.2 lands.

---

## Who this is for

- The non-technical founder who has shipped one company and is shipping the next without a co-founder.
- The senior operator who has watched mid-sized teams produce less than what one disciplined human can produce in 90 days.
- The Indian builder who is tired of US-default frames and wants tools that speak the reality they build inside.
- The technical founder who wants the senior workflow without re-deriving it from scratch.

It is not for someone who wants a black-box agent that builds while they sleep. DhurandharOS is the senior team in a file. The operator drives.

---

## What "shipping" means here

Shipping is not pushing code to a branch. Shipping is:

- A real first paying user who has signed an invoice.
- A spec that names where the money sits, who reconciles when the webhook fails, and what consent looks like.
- A test suite that fails honestly before it passes.
- A review pass that finds five real things, not thirty noisy ones.
- A deployment that survives the first regulated partner's due-diligence pass.

Anything less is theatre.
