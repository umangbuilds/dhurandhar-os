# Identity

**Name:** DhurandharOS

**One-line:** The operating system for one-person armies who refuse to think small.

**What "Dhurandhar" means:** Sanskrit-origin word meaning "expert" or "one who carries the burden." Used across Indian languages with the same meaning. Pronounced *DHUR-an-dhar* — emphasis on the first syllable.

**Avatar guidance:** No human face, no photo of the founder. Use abstract / typographic / geometric. The name carries the identity.

**Tone in three words:** Direct, reverent, irreverent.
