# Roadmap

DhurandharOS ships in three versions over 180 days. Each is independently usable.

---

## v0.1 — universal solo-operator workflow + Hiring wedge (now)

**6 Tier 1 skills** authored, customised, and maintained by DhurandharOS:

1. **Soul Keeper** — loads SOUL.md / STYLE.md / IDENTITY.md at session start, captures memory and lessons, enforces voice.
2. **Builder** — TDD-first orchestrator with plan-first discipline, destructive-command guardrails, and worktree isolation.
3. **Reviewer** — adversarial security and code review with OWASP / ASVS coverage, false-positive filtering, and routing to Tier 2 add-ons for regulatory deep-dives.
4. **PRD Writer** — money-aware spec writer that forces settlement-timing, reconciliation ownership, and consent capture before locking the spec.
5. **Idea Reality Check** — adversarial brainstorming that refuses to ship code without first paying users named.
6. **Hiring JD Writer** — Indian-context job description writer with comp bands cited from public listing data, anti-discrimination guardrails, and ESOP / FEMA compliance footers.

**1 stub** — Launch + Marketer points to Tier 2 add-ons until v0.2.

**~20 Tier 2 recommendations** — curated catalog organised by founder stage. Installed by the user from the original author. Not shipped.

**Identity layer** — SOUL.md + STYLE.md + IDENTITY.md + MEMORY.md + mission.md + lessons.md + ROADMAP.md at repo root.

**v0.1 is operator-fundamentals.** The original India-fintech wedges (DPDPA Compliance, RBI Digital Lending, UPI Payments) have been deliberately moved to v0.2 to keep v0.1 universal.

---

## v0.2 — fintech operator's brain (Day 60–90)

- **Launch + Marketer** built out from stub. Positioning, copy, channel sequencing, India launch playbook.
- **DPDPA Compliance** as a core skill (not a Tier 2 router). Itemized consent capture, DPIA workflow, breach-notification timelines, DPB / Adjudicating Officer routing.
- **RBI Digital Lending** — Digital Lending Guidelines 2022 + 2023 amendments. LSP/DLA boundaries, KFS, loan agreements, default loss guarantee constraints.
- **UPI Payments** — collect-and-pay flow, autopay mandates, dispute timelines, QR specifications, NPCI dispute resolution.

This is the version where DhurandharOS becomes India-fintech-grade. v0.1 operators who need DPDPA / RBI today should install the Tier 2 add-ons listed in RECOMMENDED.md.

---

## v0.3 — broader Indian compliance + regional voice (Day 90–180)

- Incorporation workflows (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, Section 8).
- GST / TDS handling for SaaS, marketplace, and lending models.
- POSH compliance for sub-10 and 10+ employee teams.
- ESOP grant + exercise lifecycle. FEMA implications for foreign-resident employees.
- ToS / Privacy template generators with DPDPA + IT Act + Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules.
- Hindi and regional voice variants for user-facing copy.
- Tier 2 / Tier 3 city GTM playbooks.

---

## What stays out of scope, ever

- Autonomous black-box agents.
- US-default compliance kits without Indian translation.
- Paid tiers, monetised features, gated content. MIT, free, forever.
- Competitor PSP / aggregator brand promotion.
- Film references, founder-as-celebrity framing.
